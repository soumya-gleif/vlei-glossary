[[def: delegated-aid, Delegated AIDs, delegated-aids]]

~ Autonomic Identifiers (AIDs) which are primary identifiers as defined by KERI [[ref: KERI]]. Delegated identifiers are anchored by other primary identifiers. Examples are: GLEIF Internal Delegated AID (GIDA) and GLEIF External Delegated AID (GEDA) which are delegated from the GLEIF Root AID.
